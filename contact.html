Travel Tips

  • Make sure your passport is valid for at least 6 months after your trip.
  • Check visa and entry requirements before booking flights.
  • Tell your bank you're traveling so your card isn't blocked.
  • Use a card with no foreign transaction fees.
  • Keep digital copies of your passport and bookings.
  • Consider travel insurance for international or adventure trips.
