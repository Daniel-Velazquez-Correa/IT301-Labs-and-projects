Beach Getaway

North America

Cancun, Mexico

White sand and turquoise Caribbean water, with Mayan ruins just a day trip away.

  • Must-see: Hotel Zone beaches, Isla Mujeres, Chichén Itzá
  • Activities: snorkeling, swimming in cenotes, boat tours
  • Best time: December - April

Maui, Hawaii, USA

Golden beaches, volcanic landscapes, and one of the most scenic drives in the world.

  • Must-see: Ka'anapali Beach, Road to Hana, Haleakalā sunrise
  • Activities: snorkeling at Molokini Crater, surfing, whale watching
  • Best time: April - May, September - November
Explore!!

Europe

Santorini, Greece

Whitewashed villages on volcanic cliffs above the Aegean Sea, famous for its sunsets.

  • Must-see: Oia sunset, Red Beach, Perissa black sand beach
  • Activities: caldera boat tours, swimming, cliffside dining
  • Best time: May - June, September - October
Explore!!

Amalfi Coast, Italy

Colorful cliffside towns, lemon groves, and crystal-clear Mediterranean coves.

  • Must-see: Positano, Ravello, the island of Capri
  • Activities: boat trips, swimming, hiking the Path of the Gods
  • Best time: May - June, September
Explore!!

Asia

Maldives

Overwater bungalows on private islands surrounded by coral reefs and calm lagoons.

  • Must-see: coral reefs, sandbank picnics, bioluminescent beaches
  • Activities: snorkeling, scuba diving with manta rays, sunset cruises
  • Best time: November - April
Explore!!

Bali, Indonesia

Tropical beaches, surf breaks, and cliffside temples on Indonesia's most famous island.

  • Must-see: Uluwatu Temple, Nusa Penida, Tegallalang Rice Terraces
  • Activities: surfing, snorkeling, beach clubs
  • Best time: April - October (dry season)
