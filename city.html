City Lights

North America

New York City, USA

The city that never sleeps, packed with world-famous landmarks, Broadway shows, and endless neighborhoods to explore.

  • Must-see: Central Park, Times Square, Statue of Liberty
  • Must-try: New York-style pizza, bagels
  • Best time: April - June, September - November
learn more

Mexico City, Mexico

A huge, colorful capital with Aztec history, world-class museums, and some of the best street food anywhere.

  • Must-see: Zócalo, Frida Kahlo Museum, Chapultepec Park
  • Must-try: tacos al pastor, pozole
  • Best time: March - May
learn more

Europe

Paris, France

The City of Light, known for its art, romantic streets, and sidewalk cafés.

  • Must-see: Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Montmartre
  • Must-try: croissants, crêpes
  • Best time: April - June, September - October
learn more

Barcelona, Spain

A lively coastal city famous for Gaudí's architecture, late-night dining, and Mediterranean beaches.

  • Must-see: Sagrada Família, Park Güell, La Rambla
  • Must-try: tapas, paella
  • Best time: May - June, September - October
learn more

Asia

Tokyo, Japan

A mix of neon-lit streets and quiet temples, with some of the best food in the world.

  • Must-see: Shibuya Crossing, Senso-ji Temple, Shinjuku
  • Must-try: sushi, ramen
  • Best time: March - May (cherry blossoms), October - November
learn more

Seoul, South Korea

A modern city full of ancient palaces, busy night markets, and K-pop culture.

  • Must-see: Gyeongbokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok Village, Myeongdong
  • Must-try: Korean BBQ, tteokbokki
  • Best time: April - June, September - November
