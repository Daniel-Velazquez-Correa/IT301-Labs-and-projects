Adventure & Outdoors

North America

Banff, Alberta, Canada

Turquoise lakes like Lake Louise and Moraine Lake surrounded by the Canadian Rockies.

  • Activities: hiking, canoeing, skiing
  • Difficulty: Easy - Moderate
  • Best time: June - September (hiking), December - March (skiing)
learn more

Grand Teton National Park, Wyoming, USA

Jagged mountain peaks rising over alpine lakes, with moose and bison in the valleys.

  • Activities: hiking, wildlife viewing, kayaking on Jenny Lake
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Best time: June - September
learn more

Europe

Swiss Alps, Switzerland

Snowy peaks like the Matterhorn, mountain villages, and some of the most scenic trails in Europe.

  • Activities: hiking, skiing, scenic train rides
  • Difficulty: Easy - Hard (trails for every level)
  • Best time: June - September (hiking), December - March (skiing)
learn more

Vatnajökull National Park, Iceland

Home to one of Europe's largest glaciers, with blue ice caves and waterfalls like Svartifoss.

  • Activities: guided glacier hikes, ice cave tours
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Best time: June - August (hiking), November - March (ice caves)
learn more

South America

Machu Picchu, Peru

A 15th-century Inca citadel high in the Andes, reached by train or the multi-day Inca Trail.

  • Activities: hiking, exploring ruins
  • Difficulty: Moderate - Hard (high altitude)
  • Best time: May - September (dry season)
learn more

Patagonia, Chile/Argentina

Granite peaks and massive glaciers at the southern tip of South America, including Torres del Paine.

  • Activities: trekking, glacier viewing, camping
  • Difficulty: Hard (strong winds and changing weather)
  • Best time: November - March
learn more

Asia

Mount Fuji, Japan

Japan's highest peak at 3,776 meters. Many climbers hike overnight to watch the sunrise from the summit.

  • Activities: summit climbing, lake views at Fujigoko
  • Difficulty: Moderate - Hard
  • Best time: July - early September (official climbing season)
learn more

Ha Long Bay, Vietnam

A UNESCO World Heritage Site with thousands of limestone islands rising from emerald water.

  • Activities: kayaking, cave exploring, overnight cruises
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Best time: March - May, September - November
learn more
